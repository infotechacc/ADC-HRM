# ADC HRMS – worker guide

Log in on your phone browser. Your home screen shows:

1. **Clock In / Clock Out** – appears shortly before your shift. Allow location
   access when asked. You must be at the client's address (within about 50 m).
   Your location is read only when you tap; it is not tracked.
2. **Clock Out** opens your **shift note**. Answer the questions marked *, then
   *Submit note and Clock Out*. If something fails, your note is saved as a draft.
3. **Problem signing in or out?** Use the link under the button (no GPS, wrong
   address, no signal, forgot to sign in/out). Your coordinator reviews it.
4. **Report incident** – any time during your shift, for the client you are
   supporting. Add photos, video or audio if useful. This is an internal report.
5. **My shifts** – your published roster for the next 14 days.
6. **Team messages**, **My leave** (request leave), **My profile**.

Logging out does **not** end your shift – always Clock Out.
