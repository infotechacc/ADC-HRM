# Requesting support (participants and representatives)

1. Open the link in your invitation email and set a password.
2. Go to **Support requests ▸ New request**.
3. Describe the support needed, preferred date(s) and times, and the location.
4. Follow the status: Submitted → In review → Approved → Scheduled (or Declined
   with ADC's reason). You can cancel a request until it is approved.

ADC chooses and confirms the support worker. You will not see staff records.
